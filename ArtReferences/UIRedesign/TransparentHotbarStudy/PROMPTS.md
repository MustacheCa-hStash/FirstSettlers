```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Asset: two matching transparent HUD foreground strips for First Settlers, a restrained stylized low-poly Iron Age settlement/survival game. User dislikes the old opaque stone hotbar because it looks like a flat picture pasted over a lively world. Design a much more open, see-through HUD with spatial separation, sparse edges and small selective material accents. Reference1 is the OLD OPAQUE UI: use ONLY its item silhouettes, item order and 9-slot+offhand FUNCTION; reject its heavy slab, box fills, continuous outer frame and ornament. Reference2 shows the actual game world ONLY as style context; never draw any of that landscape into the output.
Composition: wide transparent canvas approximately 2048x768. Exactly TWO horizontal hotbar rows, each comfortably within the edges, separated by a large fully transparent vertical gap. TOP row: populated demonstration. BOTTOM row: identical geometry and frame treatment with all slots EMPTY, first main slot still selected. No titles, words, labels, numbers 1–9, headings, logos, legend, backdrop, printed checkerboard or gray canvas. Both rows are isolated foreground art only, actual alpha0 outside outlines/icons, actual alpha transparency inside cells. No new scenery. No atmospheric fog, sparkles or particles.
Each row: one separate offhand cell at the left, then a clearly larger gap, then exactly9 equally sized square main slot hit areas in a single horizontal row. Spacing and scale identical across both rows. Nine main cells, not8 or10. First main slot is active: a restrained translucent slate-blue tint at roughly20% opacity and a pale ivory selection cue. Never a solid blue filled card. Other slots only have the very faint translucent treatment specified in the variant below. All gaps reveal the world. No solid backplate shared across cells. Thin dark micro-shadow under pale marks helps readability over grass.
Populated row contents in order: offhand wooden shield; main1 stoneaxe green durability bar68%, main2 woodhammer green bar84%, main3 torch12, main4 wood20, main5 stone24, main6 berries8, main7 rope2, main8 empty, main9 empty. Item art modest low-poly volumetric inventory renders, not glossy photorealism. Icons are opaque, small, crisp and floating in their hit areas; do not bake a background around any icon. Stack counts small warm ivory with dark edge bottom-right; no count1 and no keybinding numbers. Wear bars are narrow under the two tools, within their slots.
Bottom row has no item icons, wear bars or stack counts, all10 cells empty including offhand. This row must still show how empty hit areas are indicated unobtrusively.
Period/material palette limited to weathered limestone, charcoal and desaturated slate-blue selection, very subtle warm bone/brown edges. No modern holographic graphics, frosted glass, glossy gradients, gold, bulky temples or cartoony stone buttons. Calm functional professional HUD, no giant icons. The point is the world showing through the HUD. Preserve real transparency, including partially transparent cell washes, so this asset can be composited over the user's UNCHANGED screenshots.

Variant A OPEN STONE CORNERS: Each cell has ONLY four very short narrow L-shaped corner marks in muted pale limestone, 1–2px at actual HUD scale. Corners suggest an open square; long sides and center are completely absent. No full rectangle. Unselected cell interiors alpha0. Selected first main slot has a slightly stronger ivory corner set plus the faint transparent slate-blue wash. All corner marks have extremely shallow material relief, no chunky carved blocks. No connected rail or baseline. Clear transparent gaps.
```
