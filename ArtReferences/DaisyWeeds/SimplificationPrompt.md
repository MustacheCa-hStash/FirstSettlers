Petals must be plain warm ivory-white with at most ONE very faint broad cream gradient on each petal; remove ALL dark overlap shadows, dark creases, grey wedges, individual veins, brush strokes and realistic shading. Leaf: fill the leaf mostly with ONE uniform soft meadow-green color, with only a SINGLE simple pale green midrib along its center and a gentle low-contrast green gradient from one side to the other. Remove ALL secondary veins, wrinkles, painterly strokes, sharp shade facets, freckles and detailed highlights. Preserve the existing lobed leaf silhouette and short stalk. The result should look obviously simplified, clean, soft, and readable at low resolution. Do not add any objects, labels, outlines, shadows, decorations, stems below the flower, extra petals or leaves. Preserve genuine transparency, and leave the space between the flower and leaf transparent.
